## v0.1.0-beta.1

First public beta.

- Minecraft movement, combat and block building in Black Myth: Wukong.
- Prologue skipping enabled by default for new saves; hold E to try skipping later real-time scenes.
- English and Chinese setup messages and guides, with a dedicated Minecraft modpack.
- Project-drawn skin and support for custom classic 64x64 skin PNGs.
- Backups before installation; game progress and Minecraft worlds kept on uninstall.

Build, loader and offline checks passed. Fresh setup, skin appearance, story skipping and chapter transitions still need live acceptance testing. Publish as a pre-release.
