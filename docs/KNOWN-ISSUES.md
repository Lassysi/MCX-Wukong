# Known issues

English | [简体中文](KNOWN-ISSUES.zh-CN.md)

v0.2.0 is a public test release. Passing builds, loader checks and offline regression checks does not establish complete in-game acceptance.

- Some real-time cutscenes and short gathering/chest animations may show the original character or clipping. Holding E does not skip every scene. Native story transformations, including the cicada, remain native-controlled.
- Chapter changes, respawn and story recovery include fixes, but a full all-chapter playthrough has not been validated. Keep save backups for both games before installing or upgrading.
- New trading, enchanting, status effects and their combinations need broader dual-game testing. Their rules are adapted rather than exact copies of vanilla Minecraft.
- Milk and honey temporarily suppress native ongoing status damage; they do not guarantee removal of native debuffs or visuals. Invisibility has not been adapted to native enemy aggro.
- Ender pearls, elytra, falling blocks and complex collision scenes may have edge cases. Scene destruction does not bypass story gates or quest mechanisms.
- Some third-party gun models or ammunition have compatibility limits. Known broken types are excluded from the shop.
- Only the pinned game versions and dedicated modpack are supported. Bedrock, multiplayer and additional mod combinations are outside the supported configuration.
- Custom skins require classic 64×64 PNGs. Slim arms, account-skin synchronization and runtime skin replacement are unsupported.
- Fresh-machine setup, the project skin and extended dual-game sessions need further validation. Minimum hardware has not been established.

For reports, include version, chapter/location, reproduction steps, error text and relevant mod log excerpts. Do not publish account files, personal saves or complete memory dumps.
