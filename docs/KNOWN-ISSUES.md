# Known issues

English | [简体中文](KNOWN-ISSUES.zh-CN.md)

- Chapter transitions have previously crashed. Transition guards were added, but a complete cross-chapter playthrough is still pending.
- Some real-time cutscenes may show the native character or ignore hold-E skipping.
- Fresh installations, reloading and skin appearance require further in-game testing.
- Not every chapter, transformation, boss, item or third-party mod has been validated.
- Collision terrain is sampled from Black Myth. Complex steps and narrow paths can still feel uneven.
- Setup currently accepts only the pinned Black Myth, loader and Minecraft versions.

**v0.1.0-beta.1** is a public beta. Successful builds and offline checks do not confirm that every chapter and feature has been tested in-game.
